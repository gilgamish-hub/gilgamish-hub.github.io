FinReport Agent

Answers questions about company annual reports, citing the pages it used. It plans its searches, checks it found the right passages, does every calculation with a calculator tool and verifies its numbers against the report.

02LIVE · FINE-TUNED LEGALBERT

Legal Contract Auditor

Splits a contract into clauses, classifies each one into 19 clause types with a fine-tuned LegalBERT model, flags risky wording and shows which words drove each prediction.

88.4%
accuracy on held-out contracts
0.864
macro F1 (baseline 0.811)
76
test contracts never seen in training
  • 9,149 clauses from the lawyer annotations in CUAD (510 contracts), split by contract so the test set is truly unseen.
  • My first version claimed 96.8%, but its labels came from keyword rules that alone scored 95.8%. I rebuilt the labels from the real annotations and documented why.
